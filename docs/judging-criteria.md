# SharkByte 2026: Judging Criteria

## What judges score

1. **Technical complexity**: how hard the build is.
2. **Innovation**: how original the idea is.
3. **UI/UX**: how polished and usable it is.
4. **Practical utility**: whether it solves a real problem.

## How judging works

- **Live demo:** each judge comes to our table, and we get 3 minutes to show them the project.
- **Devpost submission:** a 1:30 video and the GitHub link.
- **Deadlines:** hacking ends Sun Nov 8 at 10:00 AM EST, and Devpost submission closes at 11:00 AM.
- **Sponsor and MLH challenges:** we have to opt into each one on Devpost, or we won't be considered for it.
- **Originality rule:** all code, design and assets must be made during the weekend. Open-source libraries and APIs are allowed.

## What won last year (2025)

- 10 of the 13 prize categories were sponsor or MLH challenges.
- All of the top 3 overall projects had live camera or voice demos.
- Every AI winner we checked used Gemini.
